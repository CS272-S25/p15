Welcome!

Posted on February 26, 2025 by Cole Nelson

Welcome to CS272 or CS/LIS472!

I'm excited to be your instructor for this course in which we introduce web development!

This class can be taken as CS 272 or CS/LIS 472. What's the difference?

  • CS272 is exam-based and open to everyone.
  • CS/LIS 472 is project-based and intended for Information Science students.

Both sections meet together and learn the same content. Assessment for CS272 includes a midterm and final exam. Assessment for CS/LIS 472 includes a midterm and final group project. Please see the syllabus on Canvas and/or consult with your advisor for more details on which offering is best for you.

Our first class will be on Wednesday, January 22nd from 4:00-5:15 pm at 212 Animal Science Building. A brief overview and tentative schedule can be found on Canvas, along with the tentative dates for assignments, discussions, quizzes, and exams or projects. I hope to see you all at our first class!

Sincerely,
Cole Nelson
